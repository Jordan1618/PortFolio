# Sport Program and Weekly Planning

> **My training planning, built as a PPL split with an upper-body / lower-body logic. I treat it like a small project: a goal, a plan, fixed rules, and adjustments from what really happens.**

---

## Vision & Purposes

- **Have a clear plan instead of improvising in the gym.** Fixed sessions, fixed rest times, a fixed duration (under 1h10 including warm-up).
- **Keep a structure I can follow six days a week**, with a defined session for each day.
- **Build the plan from an exercise bank**, so that every exercise has a reason to be there.

---

## The Technical Core (Method and Tools)

- **Claude** helped me draft and reorganize the documents, and I made the final choices.
- **Word documents** as the final format, with tables: muscle, type of exercise, score, sets, reps, weight.
- **A scoring system for exercises:** 5 stars = essential, 4 = very good, 3 = option to rotate. A variant is marked with an arrow and rotated every 3 to 4 weeks.
- **Rules of use:** 8 exercises per session with a balance per muscle group, keep the 5-star exercises fixed for 8 to 12 weeks, never two variants of the same exercise in one session.

---

## The PPL Split

- **Push (upper body):** chest, shoulders, triceps
- **Pull (upper body):** back, rear shoulder, biceps
- **Legs (lower body):** quadriceps, hamstrings, glutes, calves

Each exercise in the bank has a muscle, a type (compound or isolation), a score, a number of sets, a rep range and my working weight.

---

## Weekly Planning

Six sessions a week, with the lower body split in two days so it gets both strength and volume:

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Monday | Push |
| Tuesday | Legs A (strength) |
| Wednesday | Pull |
| Thursday | Legs B (volume) |
| Friday | Push |
| Saturday | Pull |

Each session has the same shape: warm-up (7 to 8 minutes), main work, a short end block. Rest is 1 minute for isolation, 1 minute 30 for compound lifts, 30 seconds in the end block. The load goes up over the weeks, and a public holiday moves a session to the next day.

---

## Problems Encountered

- **Choosing among too many exercises.** The score and the "8 per session" rule fixed this.
- **Keeping the plan realistic.** A session must fit in 1h10, so warm-up and rest times are counted in the total.
- **A long document.** A short legend of the rules at the top makes it usable in the gym.
