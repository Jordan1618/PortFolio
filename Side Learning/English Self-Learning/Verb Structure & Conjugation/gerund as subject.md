# gerund as subject

## Rule
When a verb is the subject of a sentence, use the -ing form: Automating everything is a key skill. The bare verb cannot be the subject.

## Cousins (if relevant)
| Wrong | Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Automate everything is a key skill | Automating everything is a key skill | -ing makes the verb a noun |
| Learn English take time | Learning English takes time | -ing, and takes agrees with the subject |

## The trap
French uses the infinitive as a subject: Automatiser tout est une compétence clé. English needs the -ing form.

## Rule of thumb
A verb used as a subject at the start of a sentence: add -ing.
