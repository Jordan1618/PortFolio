# present after when

## Rule
After when, as soon as, before, after, until: use the present simple, even if the meaning is future. I will do it when I have time.

## Cousins (if relevant)
| Wrong | Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|
| when I will have time | when I have time | no will in a time clause |
| if it will rain | if it rains | same rule for if |

## The trap
French uses the future after quand: quand j'aurai le temps. English keeps will for the main clause only.

## Rule of thumb
will goes in the main clause, the present goes after when, if, before, after, until.
