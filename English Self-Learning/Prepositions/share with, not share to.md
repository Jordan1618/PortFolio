# share with, not share to

## Rule
The verb share takes with before the person you share something with: share something with someone.

## Cousins (if relevant)
| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| share with | give someone a part of what you have or know | I share my notes with my team |
| give to | hand something to someone | I give the file to my colleague |
| explain to | explain something to a person | I explain the script to my colleague |

## The trap
The "to" reflex comes from verbs like give to, send to, explain to. It is the same trap as apply on vs apply to: every verb has its own fixed preposition.

## Rule of thumb
When a verb is followed by a person, check the preposition of that exact verb. Share goes with with.
