# technologic vs technological

## Rule
The adjective of technology is technological. In normal technical English, use technological.

## Cousins (if relevant)
| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| technological | linked to technology | a technological watch |
| technical | linked to a skill or a method | a technical problem |
| technologist | a person who works with technology | a lab technologist |

## The trap
I guessed the adjective instead of checking the real form. It is the same mistake as Administration vs Administrative Templates.

## Rule of thumb
Do not guess an adjective from a noun. Check the standard form, especially for -ic / -ical pairs.
